One step at a time. Check things off as you do them, your progress saves on its own.

How this works

You run three accounts posting AI-made videos for Klareva, a skincare product. You never film anything and you are never on camera. AI avatars are.

Your affiliate link sits in the bio of every account. Someone buys through it, you get paid. That is the whole business.

There are two kinds of videos and nothing else. Short lifestyle videos that grow the accounts, and long product reviews that make the money.

Every account is an Instagram and a Facebook page posting the same videos.

Lifestyle videos  ·  short  ·  about 5 minutes each

One clip. An older woman who looks far younger than her age, text over the top with her age, and the description lists what she does, with our product sitting quietly in the middle of that list. A comment keyword in the caption triggers the DM automation.

The avatar is the whole video. She plainly reads her age, but her skin looks shocking for it. That mismatch is what stops the scroll.

Product reviews  ·  long  ·  about an hour 20 each

Opens on a problem, neck or hands or under eyes, then reviews products, usually in a store. The last product is ours and it is the one that makes everything else work. This is where the money comes from.

The lanes do not mix. The Viral Radar is for review videos only. Never copy those for your lifestyle accounts.

METHOD is a set of instructions written for Claude, not for you. It teaches Claude how we take a video that already went viral and rebuild it as ours. It keeps what made the original work, the structure, the pacing, the way the hook is worded, and swaps out the parts that need to change: the body part being talked about, the four products being reviewed, and the last product, which is always Klareva.

So you do not read it. You copy the whole thing, paste it into Claude once, and then keep using that same chat every day.

Rules that never change
  • Klareva is a hydrocolloid. On camera it is the small cube. Always the winner, always last.
  • No em dashes in any spoken line. Commas only. Em dashes break Veo’s speech generation.
  • Never invent a product name. Every product has to be real and spelled exactly as on the packaging, because it gets said out loud.
Last thing

You are going to be overwhelmed on day one. Everyone is. That is not a sign this is not for you, it is just what day one feels like.

The people who make money are the ones who ask constantly. Jayden replies in a minute or two and you can hop on a call any time. The people who go quiet stall out.

Do not sit on a problem for three days. That is three days you do not get back.
