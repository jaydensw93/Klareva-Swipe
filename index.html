1  ·  How this works

You run three accounts posting AI-made videos for Klareva, a skincare product. You never film anything and you are never on camera. AI avatars are.

Your affiliate link sits in the bio of every account. Someone buys through it, you get paid. That is the whole business.

There are two kinds of videos and nothing else. Short lifestyle videos that grow the accounts, and long product reviews that make the money.

The lanes do not mix. The Viral Radar is for review videos only. Never copy those for your lifestyle accounts.

Every day after that — how videos get made

You are never writing a video from scratch. You are taking one that already worked and transplanting it.

01Find a viral video in the Viral Radar, or in the video ideas channel in Discord. Look for one that did way better than that account normally does.
02Transcribe it. Work from what is actually spoken, never the caption or on-screen text.
03Open a new Claude chat and paste in the whole METHOD document. METHOD is the file that teaches Claude how we turn other people’s videos into ours. You do not read it, you just copy all of it, paste it into Claude and hit send. Once per chat, then keep using that same chat.
04Paste in the transcript and tell Claude what to change. For example: “this video is about the face, make it about the neck instead. Film it inside a Costco. The last product should be Klareva.” It hands you back the finished script.

You need the METHOD document to do any of this.

METHOD is a set of instructions written for Claude, not for you. It teaches Claude how we take a video that already went viral and rebuild it as ours. It keeps what made the original work, the structure, the pacing, the way the hook is worded, and swaps out the parts that need to change: the body part being talked about, the four products being reviewed, and the last product, which is always Klareva.

So you do not read it. You copy the whole thing, paste it into Claude once, and then keep using that same chat every day.

Rules that never change

  • Klareva is a hydrocolloid. On camera it is the small cube. Always the winner, always last.
  • No em dashes in any spoken line. Commas only. Em dashes break Veo’s speech generation.
  • Never invent a product name. Every product has to be real and spelled exactly as on the packaging, because it gets said out loud.

Last thing

You are going to be overwhelmed on day one. Everyone is. That is not a sign this is not for you, it is just what day one feels like.

The people who make money are the ones who ask constantly. Jayden replies in a minute or two and you can hop on a call any time. The people who go quiet stall out.

Do not sit on a problem for three days. That is three days you do not get back.

Viral Radar

Every video worth copying, in one place. This is where your day starts. You do not go hunting on Instagram for ideas, you open the library and take one.

Open the Winning Content Library  →

Thousands of videos pulled from the accounts in our lane, with thumbnails, view counts and a link straight to the original. Sort by most views, most recent, most likes or most comments. It rebuilds itself twice a day, so what you see in the morning is not what you saw yesterday.

It will ask for the passcode. Same one you got when you joined. If you do not have it, ask in your chat and it gets sent to you.

Your own link is different from this one. If videos have been assigned to you personally, you were sent a link with a code on the end of it. Open that one instead and your assigned videos and scripts show up alongside the library. Keep it, it is yours and it does not change.

How to pick one

Do not just take the video with the biggest number. A video doing 400,000 views on an account where every video does 400,000 views teaches you nothing, that account is just big. What you want is the video that beat its own account.

So before you commit to one, tap through to the original and scroll that account's recent posts. If the video you picked looks like everything else they post, keep looking. If it is clearly outperforming the stuff around it, something in that video worked that does not normally work, and that is the thing worth rebuilding.

Then run it through METHOD and film it. One video, start to finish, before you go looking for the next one.
